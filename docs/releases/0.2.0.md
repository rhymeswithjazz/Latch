# Latch 0.2.0

Adjust when Latch locks without repeating the signal test.

- The new **Lock signal level** section puts the threshold slider beside signal history. Click **Save signal level** to apply it without changing your delays.
- The saved level persists between launches. Saving clears earlier lock previews so you can test the new level.
- **Settings** replaces **Open Diagnostics** in the menu, and the window is now called **Latch Settings**.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Existing Watch selection and settings are preserved.

For an update-enabled installation, choose **Check for Updates**. Otherwise, download the ZIP, quit Latch, and replace your installed app with the included Latch.app.
