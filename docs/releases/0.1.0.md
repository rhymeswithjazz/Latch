# Latch 0.1.0

Latch locks your Mac when your selected Apple Watch moves away.

- Guided calibration records your desk signal and a walk-away test.
- Automatic locking is opt-in and uses your saved threshold and delays.
- Signal history and lock events stay on your Mac.
- Sparkle checks for updates, with an optional test-build channel.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Install this first update-enabled build manually, then use Check for Updates for later releases. Existing Watch selection and settings are preserved. Watch tracking and the private macOS lock integration still require testing on your hardware.
