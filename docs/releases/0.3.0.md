# Latch 0.3.0

Start your screen saver when you walk away.

- Choose **Start Screen Saver** under **When I walk away** in Settings, then turn on the walk-away switch. **Lock Screen** remains the default.
- The menu bar command and action previews follow your choice, which survives relaunch.
- Latch waits for the screen saver to start and pauses monitoring while it runs. After dismissal, it needs a fresh nearby Watch reading before acting again.

Your macOS Lock Screen settings control when the screen saver requires a password.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Existing Watch selection, calibration, and automatic-locking preferences are preserved.

Choose **Check for Updates** in an update-enabled installation. Otherwise, download the ZIP, quit Latch, and replace your installed app with the included Latch.app.
